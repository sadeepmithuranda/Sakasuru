# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — MVP ✅ (this repository)

Authentication & sessions · onboarding · trilingual UI · dashboard · income/expense tracking · recurring payments · budgets · savings goals · Financial Health Score · Learn Money Academy (19 lessons) with quizzes · 13 calculators · basic AI assistant (verified knowledge + consent-gated insights) · gamification · notifications · admin CMS & analytics · security/privacy controls. **Pulled forward from Phase 2:** debt manager & simulator, emergency fund planner, Scam Shield, offline learning (PWA), monthly reports, student mode flag.

## Before a public launch

1. Native-speaker review of all Sinhala and Tamil text; financial-education review of every lesson.
2. Email/SMS provider → password reset, phone OTP, 2FA.
3. Legal review (PDPA 2022, terms, disclosures); penetration test.
4. Production backups, monitoring, staging environment.

## Phase 2

Community (moderated, no solicitation) · investment education centre & virtual simulator ("SIMULATION ONLY") · advanced AI insights · more lessons per level via CMS · yearly Money Story · pre/post knowledge assessments and an anonymous Impact Dashboard · React Native app reusing the shared packages.

## Phase 3

Family mode with role-based household budgets · Small Business mode (sales, receivables, payables, cash flow) · Money Today explainers with source and timestamp · voice-first AI · partner bank/open-finance integrations where legally available · schools & university programmes.

## Phase 4

Institutional dashboards (aggregate education metrics only) for universities, NGOs, employers and government programmes.
